## Why the archive exists

Three rounds of fixes produced three generations of the same potli room
(P3 → P4 → P5) and two of the same scrub (S3 → S4). Those intermediate
frames stayed useful for reference — the Figma file was still showing
P4 and S3 after the repo had moved on — but they only existed in a
scratchpad directory that does not survive the session. Anything a
conversation might point back at belongs in the repository.

The codes exist because "the potli one with the ochre wall" identifies three
different photographs.
